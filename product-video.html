Why we built it

An answer alone leaves a question

Students need to know where their reasoning changed. A final right or wrong mark cannot show that step.

Visual math editor

Enter math the way you write it

Type directly or use the math pad for fractions, roots, powers, and symbols. Gray boxes show where each value belongs.

Solve

A result with its restrictions

For this rational function, Proof Lab shows supported features and keeps x = 3 outside the domain.

f(x) = 2/(x − 3) + 1

Proof timeline

One step at a time

Reveal each transformation and its rule when you are ready. LaTeX validation and mathematical verification are shown separately.

Check work

The first error matters

A student distributes 2 to x, but misses the 4. Proof Lab points to that line and explains the mistake.

2(x + 4) = 2x + 8

Ghost steps

Missing reasoning stays visible

Skipped valid moves can be reconstructed. A corrected continuation carries its own label, separate from the student's submitted work.

Generate

A fresh problem, ready to try

Choose a supported topic and set the difficulty from 0.000 to 1.000. A seed makes each problem reproducible.

Graph

See the curve change

Plot functions, pan, zoom, or choose logarithmic axes and a polar grid. The graph is numerical exploration.

Graph art

Pictures become math to explore

Image import draws an approximation with waves, lines, or colored regions. Students can refine it and enter the daily graph-art contest.

The engine

A check before “verified”

Proof Lab parses the input, applies a supported math rule, and replays a certificate against the result.

Difficulty and coverage

Three routing tiers

Difficulty helps select a starting tier. The same certificate check decides whether a supported answer can be called verified.

Selected Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and Precalculus rules are supported. The tool does not claim full course coverage.
