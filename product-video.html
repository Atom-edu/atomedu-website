Why we built it

An answer alone leaves a question

Students need to know where their reasoning changed. A final right or wrong mark cannot show that step.

Solve

A result with its reasoning

Enter an expression in the visual math editor. Proof Lab shows supported results, restrictions, and a step-by-step timeline.

Check work

The first error matters

A student distributes 2 to x, but misses the 4. Proof Lab points to that line and explains the mistake.

2(x + 4) = 2x + 8

Ghost steps

Missing reasoning stays visible

Skipped steps can be reconstructed when the transition holds. A corrected continuation carries its own label, separate from the student's submitted work.

Generate

A fresh problem, ready to try

Choose a supported topic and set the difficulty from 0.000 to 1.000. A seed makes each generated problem reproducible.

Graph

See the curve change

Plot functions, pan, zoom, or choose a polar grid. Image import can turn pictures into math waves for the daily art contest. The separate rule engine checks supported proofs.

The engine

A check before “verified”

Proof Lab parses the input, applies a supported math rule, and replays a certificate against the result. Difficulty chooses a route through three tiers.

Unsupported problems are labeled. The graph is numerical and does not stand in for a proof.
